# Jackage invariants

Preserve user source and unrelated changes.
Use the repository's pinned Zig wrapper.
Record real red-green evidence for product behavior.
Do not count mocks as native isolation evidence.
Do not count skipped tests as passed tests.
Do not weaken release policy silently.
Keep credentials outside recipes and general build actions.
Keep caches bounded and outside source synchronization.
Do not publish or change host security without explicit authorization.
Keep `.jackage-delivery/state/SESSION_HANDOFF.md` current.
